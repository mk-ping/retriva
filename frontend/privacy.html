Who we are

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, your email address, and a hashed version of your password. We never store your password in readable form.
  • Content you add: the files and web pages you upload, the text extracted from them, and a numeric index of that text used for search.
  • Your activity: your questions, the answers you receive, the sources used, and any thumbs up or down you give.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the service: to sign you in, to search your documents, and to write answers to your questions. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Who else sees it

  • Language model provider: to write an answer, the passages found in your documents and your recent conversation are sent to [LLM PROVIDER, currently Groq]. Your whole library is not sent, only the passages relevant to the question. Please read that provider's own privacy terms.
  • Search index: the numeric index is created on our own server and is not sent to anyone.
  • Hosting: your data is stored with [HOSTING PROVIDER AND REGION].
  • Your browser also loads fonts and scripts from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which can see your IP address when you visit.

Web pages you add

When you add a web page by address, our server downloads that page on your behalf and stores a copy in your knowledge base.

Cookies and local storage

We keep a sign-in token in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How long we keep it

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting a document removes its text and index. Deleting a knowledge base removes all its documents, chats and index. [DESCRIBE HOW A USER CAN DELETE THEIR WHOLE ACCOUNT, or how to ask you to. There is no delete-account button yet.]
